Privacy Policy

Information on the Processing of Your Data

This page was originally written in German. Translations are provided for informational purposes only; the German version is legally binding.

Data Controller: LinguaFlow e.U., Email: hello@linguaflow.app

1. Collection and Processing of Personal Data

When using the LinguaFlow app and website, the following data may be collected:

  • Name, email address, and account information
  • Usage data (language settings, learning progress)
  • Payment and subscription data
  • Device information and technical log data

2. Purpose of Data Processing

  • Provision and improvement of the app and website
  • Processing of subscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Error analysis and optimization of the app
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Communication regarding support and feedback requests
  • Voluntary feedback within the app (responses to short surveys and optional free-text comments): We store this information without linking it to your account—without your name or email address. Survey responses are also included anonymously in usage statistics (TelemetryDeck).

3. Third-Party Services (Recipients of Your Data)

We use the following services. The data they receive depends on the specific feature being used:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, App Check, Crashlytics): Login, account data, usage quotas, crash and diagnostic data, as well as text you share online (see Section 4). Our own server runs on this platform.
  • Google Play Billing & Apple In-App Purchase: Processing of subscriptions. Payment data remains with Google or Apple.
  • RevenueCat: Management of subscription status (user ID, purchase history, device ID).
  • Anthropic (Claude): Word-for-word decoding of entered, scanned, or dictated text, as well as AI dialogue. Only the text is transmitted; no account data is shared.
  • OpenAI: AI practice texts, text recognition for scanned pages in non-Latin scripts (photos are processed only for recognition and are not stored), and text-to-speech in AI dialog.
  • ElevenLabs: Reading texts aloud (text-to-speech).
  • DeepL and Google Cloud Translation: Translation of sentences and words.
  • TelemetryDeck: Anonymized usage statistics (app interactions, app version, device type). No email address, no text.
  • MailerLite: Newsletters, only after explicit consent (double opt-in).
  • Google Workspace (Gmail): Sending emails, e.g., reminders before the end of the trial period, only with consent.
  • The website’s web hosting provider.

Voice recordings made using the dictation feature are converted to text by your operating system’s speech recognition software (Apple or Google); only the recognized text is sent to our servers and the services listed above.

For providers outside the EU, data protection is ensured by the EU Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. Sharing Texts Online

You can share a decoded text via a link (“Share Online”). This only happens if you initiate it yourself: The app explains what will happen before uploading and only uploads the text once you tap “Create Link.”

  • What we store in this process: the shared text with the word-for-word translation and the paraphrased sentence translations, along with any transcription and reading aids, the two languages, the title of the text (if available), the beginning of the text (the first 80 characters, so you can recognize the entry in your list), the time of sharing, and your account ID (if using the app without an account: the anonymous ID of your app installation). We do not store your name, email address, or audio recordings. We need the identifier to limit the number of links per day and to show you your list. It is not included with the link.
  • Purpose: We provide the text associated with the link so that the person you share the link with can open it in the LinguaFlow app or in a browser.
  • Who can read the text: Anyone who knows the link. The link contains a long, random identifier and is not indexed by search engines. Therefore, do not share texts that contain personal or confidential information.
  • Link preview: When you share a link in a messaging app, the app displays a preview showing the title and the beginning of the text. To do this, the app retrieves this information from our server; some messaging apps do this via their own servers. New previews no longer show a deleted link; a preview that has already been displayed remains in the chat.
  • Who can see your list: only you. Our server delivers the list only to the account that shared the texts (if used without an account: to the same app installation). Others cannot see your list or delete your links.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you provide by creating the link.
  • Retention period and deletion: The shared text is automatically deleted after 90 days (technical deletion may take up to three days; the text is no longer accessible after 90 days). You can delete a shared text yourself at any time before then: In the app, under “My Shared Links” (in Settings and in the text view menu), you’ll find all links that are still valid. If you tap “Delete Link” there, we’ll immediately remove the text from our server; the link will no longer work for anyone after that. Anyone who has already opened the text or saved it to their own app will retain that copy; we have no control over this. If you delete your account, your shared texts will also be deleted. You can email us at any time at hello@linguaflow.app and provide the link, and we will delete the text.
  • Storage location: Google Firebase (Google Cloud), data center in the EU (Belgium).
  • Webpage in the browser (share.linguaflow.app/s/ and linguaflow.app/s/): The page loads the shared text from our server. It does not set any cookies and does not use any analytics or advertising services. The text is read aloud by your device’s or browser’s text-to-speech feature; we do not receive any data in the process. Some browsers use online voices provided by their manufacturer for this purpose; in such cases, the manufacturer processes the text being read aloud. We serve the address share.linguaflow.app via Google Firebase Hosting; the information provided above regarding the website’s web hosting also applies to linguaflow.app.
  • Report Content: Anyone who finds illegal or abusive content via a link can report it using the “Report Content” option on the page or by emailing hello@linguaflow.app. We will review the report and remove the text if necessary.

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

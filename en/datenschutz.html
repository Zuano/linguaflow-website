Privacy Policy

Information on the Processing of Your Data

This page was originally written in German. Translations are provided for informational purposes only; the German version is legally binding.

As of November 2026

Data Controller: LinguaFlow e.U., Owner Christian Nouza, Linzer Straße 17, 4100 Ottensheim, Austria, Email: hello@linguaflow.app

1. Collection and Processing of Personal Data

When using the LinguaFlow app and website, the following data may be collected:

  • Name, email address, and account information
  • Usage data (language settings, learning progress)
  • Payment and subscription data
  • Device information and technical log data

2. Purpose of Data Processing

  • Provision and improvement of the app and website
  • Processing of subscriptions and in-app purchases
  • Error analysis and optimization of the app
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Communication regarding support and feedback requests
  • Voluntary feedback within the app (responses to short surveys and optional free-text comments): We store this information without linking it to your account—without your name or email address. Survey responses are also included anonymously in usage statistics (TelemetryDeck).

3. Third-Party Services (Recipients of Your Data)

We use the following services. The data they receive depends on the specific feature being used:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, App Check, Crashlytics): Login information, account data, usage quotas, crash and diagnostic data, as well as text you share online, including any accompanying audio files (see Section 4). Our own server runs on this platform. The database is located in Germany (Berlin), server functions are hosted in Belgium, and Google processes the sign-in (authentication) in the U.S. Crash reports (Crashlytics) contain a random installation ID, device model, and system version, but not your account; Google stores them for 90 days.
  • Google Play Billing & Apple In-App Purchase: Processing of subscriptions. Payment data remains with Google or Apple.
  • Paddle.com Market Ltd (London, United Kingdom): Sales and payment processing for purchases on linguaflow.app (LinguaFlow Pro for the apps and the browser extension). Paddle acts as the seller and is the independent data controller for this data. Paddle receives your email address, country, payment information, and LinguaFlow account ID so that the Pro version can be assigned to the correct account. We receive the purchase and subscription status from Paddle, but not the full payment details. Paddle’s privacy policy: paddle.com/legal/privacy.
  • RevenueCat: Management of subscription status (user ID, purchase history, device ID).
  • Anthropic (Claude): Word-for-word decoding of entered, scanned, or dictated text, as well as AI dialogue. Only the text (or the photo when scanning) is transmitted; no account data is shared. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models and typically deletes it after 30 days.
  • OpenAI: AI practice texts, text recognition for scanned pages in non-Latin scripts (the photo is processed only for recognition purposes and is not stored), and speech synthesis in AI dialogue. OpenAI does not use this data for training and deletes it after 30 days at the latest.
  • ElevenLabs: Text-to-speech. The text and technical connection data such as the IP address are transmitted; no account information is transmitted. We have disabled the use of this data for training ElevenLabs models.
  • DeepL and Google Cloud Translation: Translation of sentences and words.
  • TelemetryDeck: Pseudonymized usage statistics (the device ID is irreversibly transformed—hashed—twice; we cannot trace it back to you; app interactions, app version, device type). No email address, no text.
  • MailerLite: Newsletters, only with explicit consent (double opt-in).
  • Google Workspace (Gmail): Sending emails, e.g., a reminder before the end of the trial period, only with your consent. If you purchase a subscription or a one-time plan on linguaflow.app, we will also send you—without separate consent, as this is part of the contract—a reminder before each renewal or before expiration (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Website and Purchase Page

  • Hosting: The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit the site, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages (legitimate interest in secure operation, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Font: The “Inter” font is hosted on our own website and is loaded from there. No data is transferred to Google or other font providers in this process.
  • Audience Measurement: Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare Inc., USA) counts page views; according to Cloudflare, this is done without cookies and without identifying individual visitors (legitimate interest in improving the website, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Login on the Purchase Page: On linguaflow.app/pro/kaufen, you log in with your LinguaFlow account (Google Firebase Authentication). If you select “Sign in with Apple” or “Sign in with Google,” the sign-in process is handled by Apple or Google, respectively; in this case, we receive your email address and an account identifier (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Payment window: The purchase page loads the Paddle payment window (Paddle.js). In doing so, Paddle receives technical data such as your IP address and the information you enter in the payment window (see Paddle above).

Dictation feature: When you use the microphone, your operating system’s speech recognition software converts the recording into text—Apple’s speech recognition on the iPhone and Google’s on Android. The voice recording may be transmitted to Apple or Google, respectively; their respective privacy policies apply. Only the recognized text is transmitted to our servers and the services mentioned above.

Transfer to Countries Outside the EU

  • USA, providers under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (EU Commission Adequacy Decision): Google (Firebase, Workspace, Cloud Translation), ElevenLabs, Cloudflare, GitHub.
  • United States, protected by the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses: Anthropic, OpenAI, RevenueCat.
  • United Kingdom (EU Commission Adequacy Decision): Paddle.
  • Within the EU: TelemetryDeck (Germany/Netherlands), MailerLite (Ireland, servers in the EU), DeepL (Germany).

You can request a copy of the Standard Contractual Clauses at hello@linguaflow.app.

4. Sharing Texts Online

You can share a decoded text via a link (“Share Online”). This only happens if you initiate it yourself: The app explains what will happen before uploading and only uploads the text once you tap “Create Link.”

  • What we store in this process: the shared text with the word-for-word translation and the paraphrased sentence translations, along with any transcription and reading aids, the two languages, the title of the text (if available), the beginning of the text (the first 80 characters, so you can recognize the entry in your list), the time of sharing, and your account ID (if using the app without an account: the anonymous ID of your app installation). We do not store your name, your email address, or recordings of your voice. We need the identifier to limit the number of links per day and to show you your list. It is not sent along with the link.
  • Audio file: To ensure that the shared text can be read aloud in the browser using the same voice as in the app, we also store the audio file of the text being read aloud (a computer-generated voice, not a recording of you) and, for each word, the time at which it begins in the audio. If there is no audio for your text yet, the app will generate it when you share it; to do this, the text is transmitted to our service provider, ElevenLabs, just as it is when read aloud in the app (see Section 3). While the audio is being prepared, you can also select “Continue without audio”; in that case, we’ll only share the text.
  • Purpose: We make the text available via the link so that the person you share it with can open it in the LinguaFlow app or in a browser.
  • Who can read the text: Anyone who knows the link; if an audio file is available, they can also listen to the text. The link contains a long, random identifier and is not indexed by search engines. Therefore, do not share texts that contain personal or confidential information.
  • Link preview: When you share a link in a messaging app, the app displays a preview showing the title and the beginning of the text. To do this, the app retrieves this information from our server; some messaging apps do this via their own servers. New previews no longer show a deleted link; a preview that has already been displayed remains in the chat.
  • Who can see your list: only you. Our server delivers the list only to the account that shared the texts (if used without an account: to the same app installation). Others cannot see your list or delete your links.
  • Legal basis: your consent, which you provide by creating the link.
  • Retention period and deletion: The shared text is automatically deleted after 90 days (technical deletion may take up to three days; the text is no longer accessible after 90 days). You can delete a shared text yourself at any time before it expires: In the app, under “My Shared Links” (in Settings and in the text view menu), you’ll find all links that are still valid. If you tap “Delete Link” there, we’ll immediately remove the text from our server; the link will no longer work for anyone after that. Anyone who has already opened the text or saved it to their own app will retain that copy; we have no control over that. If you delete your account, your shared texts will also be deleted. The audio file is always deleted along with the text: after 90 days, when you tap “Delete Link,” and when you delete your account. You can email us at any time at hello@linguaflow.app and provide the link, and we’ll delete the text.
  • Storage location: Google Firebase (Google Cloud), data center in the EU (Belgium). The audio file is stored in Google Firebase’s file storage (Cloud Storage), also in Belgium.
  • Webpage (share.linguaflow.app/s/ and linguaflow.app/s/): The page loads the shared text from our server. It does not set any cookies and does not use any analytics or advertising services. If there is an audio file associated with the text, the page plays it; the file comes directly from Google Firebase’s file storage (firebasestorage.googleapis.com), which processes technical data such as your IP address for this purpose. If there is no audio file, your device’s or browser’s text-to-speech feature reads the text aloud; we do not receive any data in this case. Some browsers use online voices provided by their manufacturer for this purpose; in such cases, the manufacturer processes the text being read aloud. We serve the address share.linguaflow.app via Google Firebase Hosting; the information provided above regarding the website’s web hosting applies to linguaflow.app as well.
  • Reporting Content: Anyone who finds illegal or abusive content via a link can report it using the “Report Content” option on the page or by emailing hello@linguaflow.app. We will review the report and remove the text if necessary.

5. Legal Basis

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): Account, registration, guest week, decoding, scanning, dictation, AI dialogue, read-aloud, history and books in the cloud, subscription and purchase, reminders before renewal or expiration of a web subscription, support.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR): Newsletter, email reminder before the end of the trial period. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): Retention of purchase and billing data.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics (interest in a bug-free, user-friendly app), protection against misuse of the free trial week, secure operation, and website traffic measurement. You may object to this at any time.

Without an email address or account, we cannot activate Pro for you on multiple devices; the remaining information is optional.

6. Data Retention

  • Account, history, books, and settings: until you delete them or your account. When you delete your account in the app, we remove the associated data from our servers, from RevenueCat, and from MailerLite. This excludes data related to a web subscription via Paddle (see next point).
  • Guest week without an account: up to 30 days after the start; after that, Google deletes the anonymous account.
  • Web subscription via Paddle (email address provided at purchase, account ID, subscription status): for the duration of the subscription, then 7 years (tax retention requirement, § 132 BAO). An active web subscription is not terminated by account deletion—cancel it in the Paddle customer portal. Invoices for purchases made in the App Store or on Google Play are retained by Apple or Google, respectively.
  • Automatic emails (e.g., reminders): The email is automatically deleted 90 days after it is sent, or immediately upon account deletion.
  • Texts shared online: 90 days; see Section 4.
  • Support emails: 3 years after the request is closed.
  • Crash reports: 90 days (with Google).

6a. Minimum Age

To create your own account and provide consent, you must be at least 14 years old (Section 4(4) of the DSG). For purchases, the statutory minimum age for contracts applies.

Automated decision-making within the meaning of Art. 22 of the GDPR (including profiling) does not take place.

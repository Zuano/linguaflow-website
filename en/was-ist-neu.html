What's New in LinguaFlow

All updates at a glance—the latest first. LinguaFlow is constantly being developed.

Here you can see what’s been added with each update and what we’ve improved. Each entry lists the version number of the iOS app and the Android app that included the new feature.

Version 3.1 iOS 3.1 September 2026

Check out what’s new anytime—right in the settings.

New

  • “What’s New” in Settings: There, you can check at any time to see what the latest updates have brought—with a link to this update history.
  • Quick overview after every update: After an update, the app shows you once what’s new—in your language.

Improved

  • Japanese: The voice in the learning path now pronounces the particles は/へ correctly as “wa” and “e.”
  • Simplified subscription selection: 3 months or 1 year—the annual subscription is preselected.
  • Minor bug fixes.
Version 3.0 iOS 3.0 Android 1.7.0 / 1.8.0 September 2026

Free classics, try it out without an account, and an app that says “you” in 31 languages.

New

  • Public domain books: Browse free classics in your target language right in the app and read them with sentence-by-sentence decoding.
  • Try it without registering: Decode, have text read aloud, read books, and talk to your AI partner for a whole week—you don’t need to create an account until you’re ready. Everything you’ve done as a guest will be saved.
  • Sign in with Apple on Android (1.8.0): Your Apple account from your iPhone or iPad now works on Android too—including your subscription.

Improved

  • All text in the app is now translated into all 31 languages—in a consistent, friendly tone, and we address you informally throughout.
  • Minor bug fixes.
Version 2.8.3 iOS 2.8.3 Android 1.5.7 September 2026

Japanese now sounds right.

Improved

  • Japanese: The voice now reads every word correctly—in the learning path, when decoding your own texts, and in the book reader. To do this, it uses the syllabic script (kana) for each word; the kanji, romanization, and meaning remain on the screen.
  • In the book reader, Japanese displays one card per word instead of an entire paragraph.
  • Minor improvements.
Version 2.8.1 iOS 2.8.1 Android 1.5.5 September 2026

Experience decoding right from the start—and every decoding has a name.

New

  • Example sentence in the onboarding: A short sentence shows you right from the start how decoding works—word by word, for you to discover on your own.
  • Decodings with titles: Each decoding is automatically given a short title in your language and can be renamed.
  • Learning Path with English as the Starting Language: The learning path is now also available with English as the starting language—for all target languages.
Version 2.8 iOS 2.8 Android 1.5.4 August 2026

Text-to-speech that follows along with the text word for word.

New

  • Press and hold a word: Tapping and holding a word plays only that word—slowly and clearly.

Improved

  • Text-to-speech and the text now sync exactly word for word—the spoken word is always highlighted.
  • Several improvements to text-to-speech.
Version 2.7.2 iOS 2.7.2 Android 1.5.3 August 2026

Stability.

Improved

  • Transcription (phonetic transcription) during decoding for languages with non-Latin scripts.
  • The playback loop no longer stops on its own.
  • Decoding of long texts no longer fails; text-to-speech in Thai in book mode has been fixed; extra-long word cards remain on the screen.
Version 2.6.1 iOS 2.6.1 August 2026

Improved

  • Fixes a bug that made the app unusable on the iPad after the update.
Version 2.6 iOS 2.6 Android 1.5.1 August 2026

The learning path speaks your language.

New

  • Learning Path in your native language: If you’re learning German or English, word-for-word definitions, chapter titles, learning objectives, and exercises appear in your own language—for 31 languages.

Improved

  • When using the read-aloud feature in the Learning Path, the loading circle kept spinning even though the audio was already playing—fixed.
Version 2.5 iOS 2.5 Android 1.5.0 August 2026

Major update—more new features than ever before.

New

  • Learning Path in 32 languages: Guided stories with word-for-word decoding, read-aloud audio, and exercises—including a pronunciation guide for non-Latin scripts.
  • AI Conversation Partner: Practice real conversations—every response comes with word-for-word translation and audio.
  • Series, XP & Daily Goal: Stay motivated and track your progress.
  • Fresh design: The new home screen puts decoding, scanning, and dictation at your fingertips.

Improved

  • More natural, native-sounding text-to-speech voices in many languages.
  • Bug fixes and improvements.
Version 2.4 iOS July 2026

Try it free for 30 days and get an app that speaks your language.

New

  • Try it free for 30 days: The annual subscription now includes a full month free (previously 14 days)—enough time to try out all the Pro features at your own pace.
  • Reminder before the trial period ends: If you’d like, we’ll send you a reminder via message or email before your free month ends—no unpleasant surprises.
  • 9 new app languages: The user interface is now available in 30 languages—fully translated, from onboarding to settings.

Improved

  • All screens are fully localized—including hidden text and error messages.
  • Anonymous, privacy-friendly usage statistics help us improve the app in targeted ways—without ads or tracking.
Version 2.3.1 iOS July 2026

A small but important update for the onboarding process.

Improved

  • When selecting your native language and target language, all 25 supported languages are now available (previously only a selection of the most important ones)—including country flags.
  • Minor bug fixes and improvements.
Version 2.3 iOS July 2026

The major update—LinguaFlow becomes a complete learning platform.

New

  • Read and understand books: Import EPUB books and read them in your target language—each word translated individually, with natural AI pronunciation.
  • Streaks, XP, and Daily Goals: Earn experience points, keep your learning streak going, and track your progress right on the Home Screen widget.
  • Synchronization across all devices: Translations, books, reading progress, and settings are automatically available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac—in real time and even offline.
  • Translate documents: Upload PDF, Word (.docx), OpenOffice (.odt), and Markdown files and have them translated word for word. No character limit for Pro users.
  • Voice input and camera scan: Speak text or scan it directly with the camera—it’s transcribed and translated instantly.
  • New design and mascot “Lingu”: A friendly onboarding experience, a more modern interface, and lots of thoughtful details.
  • New subscription model: Three fair Pro options—weekly, 3-month, or annual.

Improved

  • Word-for-word translation for French, Italian, and Spanish is significantly more accurate (e.g., “j’ai,” “n’est,” “dell’acqua”)—even hyphenated words like “Est-ce” are translated correctly.
  • Longer texts supported: up to 15,000 characters per translation.
  • Smoother scrolling and more precise word highlighting when reading aloud—the playback position is accurately remembered, even with long texts.
  • British English as the target language works flawlessly; fully stable on iOS 26.
  • Account deletion in accordance with the GDPR—all data is completely deleted upon request. No ads, no trackers.
Version 2.0 for iOS in early 2026

The foundation of LinguaFlow as you know it today.

  • Word-by-word decoding of any text in over 30 languages—inspired by the Birkenbihl method.
  • AI-generated practice texts on the topic of your choice, tailored to your language level (A1–C2).
  • Natural text-to-speech voices with adjustable speed—from a slower starting pace to native-speaker speed.

Withdrawal & Refunds

How to withdraw from a purchase and get your money back

This page was originally written in German. This English version is provided for convenience only; the German version is legally binding.

Last updated: November 2026

This page helps you withdraw from a purchase. Who your contractual partner is and how the withdrawal works depends on where you made the purchase.

1. Purchase on linguaflow.app or in the browser extension

Your contractual partner for the purchase and payment is Paddle.com Market Ltd (“Paddle”). Paddle sells LinguaFlow Pro as the merchant (Merchant of Record), issues the invoice and handles payment and refunds. You therefore exercise your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal towards Paddle. Paddle’s Buyer Terms (section 15, for consumers in the EU) and Paddle’s Refund Policy apply.

This applies equally to subscriptions and one-time options.

2. How to withdraw

You have three ways. The deadline is met if you send your withdrawal before the 14 days have expired.

  • Online in the Paddle customer portal (easiest): Enter the email address you paid with. You will receive a sign-in link and can then choose the option to withdraw from the contract (“Vertrag widerrufen” in the German portal). You will also find the link to the customer portal in every email from Paddle.
  • Directly with Paddle: via paddle.net or by email to help@paddle.com, ideally with your Paddle order or invoice number.
  • By email to us: to hello@linguaflow.app, e.g. “I hereby withdraw from my contract for LinguaFlow Pro concluded on …”. We accept your withdrawal, forward it to Paddle immediately and confirm receipt to you. This is also the right way if your purchase is not shown in the customer portal.

3. What happens after the withdrawal

Your Pro access ends. Paddle refunds all payments for this purchase, no later than 14 days after receiving your withdrawal, using the same payment method you paid with, and at no cost to you. Depending on your bank or card provider, it may take a few days until you see the credit. Paddle will send you an email about it.

4. Model withdrawal form

You do not have to use this form; an informal message is sufficient. If you like, copy the text, fill it in and send it by email.

5. If something goes wrong

  • Should a one-time option nevertheless be renewed due to an error, we will refund that payment.
  • We always try to solve technical problems together first – just get in touch at hello@linguaflow.app.

6. Cancelling instead of withdrawing

If you only want to end a subscription so that it no longer renews, cancel it online around the clock in the Paddle customer portal (link in every email from Paddle and at linguaflow.app/pro/kuendigen) or by email to us. Paddle confirms the cancellation by email. Access remains until the end of the paid term, after which nothing more is charged; the current term is not refunded pro rata. One-time options end automatically and do not need to be cancelled.

7. Purchase in the Apple App Store or on Google Play

If you bought Pro in the iOS or Android app, Apple or Google is your contractual partner. Cancellation, withdrawal and refunds are then handled via the respective store and according to its rules; we cannot refund such purchases. Instructions can be found at linguaflow.app/pro/kuendigen.
